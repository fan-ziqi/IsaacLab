MicroDuck local USD assets

microduck_walk.usd, microduck_allcollisions.usd, and microduck_rollers.usd
derive from Pollen Robotics' MicroDuck models:
https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl
Revision: d424a0c899f6b33cbd3daeb279913134349c0b63

Source MJCFs: src/mjlab_microduck/robot/microduck/robot_walk.xml,
robot_allcollisions.xml, and robot_allcollisions_rollers.xml, including their
referenced meshes. The USDs originated in the supplied 2026-09-09 USD export.

The previous export repairs and MjcJointAPI authoring were retained. On
2026-10-02 these local copies received 14 NewtonBamDriveAPI actuator prims,
positive Newton joint friction and SI passive damping from the XL330 m6 fit.
The fit is from Rhoban BAM revision 62bd8ce12154340be97e06f7f41a0ca8f116d967.
The unused USD position-drive gains were set to zero. Masses, inertias,
transforms, geometry, collision settings, armatures and joint limits were preserved.

The files remain local and uncommitted pending asset-server publication.

The three *_backlash.usd files add the serial gear-play model from the original
MicroDuck backlash export: fourteen ±1-degree hinges, 0.01 N.m.s/rad passive
hinge damping, 0.001 kg.m^2 hinge armature, and lightweight intermediate links.
Servo position feedback includes the corresponding passive hinge; the BAM
prim records newton:hasBacklash=1. Original body, mesh, collider, and wheel
properties were retained. This authoring was applied locally on 2026-10-02.
The all-collisions backlash USD explicitly filters each lower leg against its
own sole, preserving the original adjacent-body exclusions after the dummy
links are inserted. Walking and roller variants have no active collider pairs
that lose their original adjacency exclusion.
